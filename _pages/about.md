I am an MPhil student in **Data Science and Analytics** at **The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)**. My research interests lie in **AI for data science and optimization**, with applications in transportation and aviation.

I received my bachelor's degree in Transportation (Air Traffic Management) from the **Civil Aviation Flight University of China**. My undergraduate research focused on computer vision and its applications in aviation, including object detection, airport surface monitoring, and UAV-based wildfire inspection.

### Research Interests

- **AI & optimization** — data-driven decision-making and decision-focused learning.
- **Urban air mobility** — low-altitude environments, infrastructure planning, and transportation systems.
- **Air Traffic Management** — airspace operations, air traffic flow management, and aviation safety.
